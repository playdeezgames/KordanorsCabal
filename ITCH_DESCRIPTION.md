## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/KordanorsCabal/

**ABOUT**

The town of Zooperdan has a problem. A few years ago a cabal moved into the abandoned church, and people have been going down to the cellar and not coming back. The village pooled its money and hired you, a total n00b, to put an end to the Cabal of Kordanor. Good luck!

Kordanor's Cabal is a menu-driven dungeon crawler drawn the way a VIC-20 would have drawn it: a 22 by 23 grid of chunky characters, in PETSCII colours, stretched wide like a real 1980s screen. There is no mouse cursor to aim. Everything is a column of choices, and the whole game is played with six commands: up, down, left, right, confirm, cancel.

**WHAT YOU DO**

- Walk the town square and talk to its residents: the Elder, the Innkeeper (who has a rat problem), "Honest" Dan, the Black Mage, the Blacksmith, a nihilist healer, a constable, a drunk, and a chicken.
- Buy and sell gear. Repair it. Read the books. Learn what the shards of the elements do.
- Descend through five 11 by 11 mazes under the church. Every dead end is locked, every level has a boss, and every boss has the key to the next. The deeper you go, the nastier the cult gets: elites, acolytes, bishops, and bosses that are more than a big goblin.
- Fight. Each die you roll only counts when it shows a six, so a good sword matters far more than a good level. Enemies hit back, some of them in the mind, and at 0 MP you panic, drop everything and wake up in the town square.
- Mind your hunger. It is a slow clock, but it is a clock.
- Bring home the four shards of the elements, read the notes the cult left lying around, and find out what the Elder really wants with Kordanor's horns.

Every world is generated once from a seed (shown on the Status page; use "Start with seed..." to replay one) and saved whole, so Continue picks up exactly where you left off.

**CONTROLS**

Arrow keys to move through menus, Space or Enter to confirm, Escape or Backspace to cancel. On a phone or tablet play in landscape: there is an on-screen pad, and you can also tap the rows.

**STATE OF THE GAME**

This is an old stream project of mine, picked up again, and now it is finished. The story has an end: all 25 lore notes are written, the Elemental Orb can be made, Kordanor can be reached, and what you do with his horns decides which ending you get. The difficulty was retuned so the middle of the dungeon stays dangerous.

**ABOUT THE BUILD**

Originally written in VB.NET on stream in 2022. This web version is a rewrite in Odin (compiled to WebAssembly), written with the help of Claude Code, and checked against recordings of the original game so that it plays the same. The pictures and sounds are the originals; the theme music is new, composed in the Anvil of SPLORR!!, TheGrumpyGameDev's own sound tools.
