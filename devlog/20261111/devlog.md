# Kordanor's Cabal has an ending

*Draft for November 11, 2026, written by an AI agent (Claude Code) for TheGrumpyGameDev to edit and post. If posted by an agent, end it with the AI-agent line below.*

When the browser version went up in October, the dungeon and the town worked but the story stopped short: most of the lore notes were placeholders, and the door to the last boss could not be opened. That is fixed. The game can be finished now.

## The story

- **The Elemental Orb.** Bring the Air, Earth, Fire and Water Shards to the Elder and he makes the orb that opens the Level V boss room. The Fire Shard finally spawns, in the Level III boss room.
- **Kordanor and his horns.** Beat Kordanor and take his horns back to town. What happens there depends on what you choose, and on how much you read on the way down. There are two endings.
- **All 25 lore notes are written.** They are scattered through the dungeon as notes, and taken together they tell you more about this town than the Elder does.

## The difficulty

The old balance front-loaded the danger: by the middle of level II good gear made you nearly safe. It is spread out now.

- Gear spawns are spread across the levels, so the kit grows with the dungeon.
- Elites, acolytes, bishops and priests replace the weaker monsters on levels II to V, and bishops attack the mind.
- The bosses are set pieces: the Acolyte Warden, the Bone Priest, the Flame Bishop and his Cult Guards, and a harder Cabal Leader and Kordanor.
- Chainmail and platemail raise your defence cap, so armour keeps mattering.
- The Elder trains stat points for money, which gives your gold somewhere to go.
- Marten's free heal needs 40 steps to recharge. The Water Shard shares that rest, and the Fire Shard costs 2 mana.
