        PROLOG        
                      
THE TOWN OF ZOOPERDAN 
HAS FALLEN UPON HARD  
TIMES. A FEW YEARS    
AGO, THE CABAL OF     
KORDANOR MOVED INTO A 
NEARBY ABANDONED      
CHURCH. AT FIRST, NO  
ONE THOUGHT MUCH OF   
THE STRANGE CABAL     
MEMBERS, BUT SOON THE 
VILLAGERS STARTED     
DISAPPEARING ONE BY   
ONE. SO THEY POOLED   
THEIR MONEY AND SET   
ABOUT HIRING SOMEONE  
TO GO IN THERE AND PUT
AN END TO THE CABAL   
ONCE AND FOR ALL! THEY
HIRED YOU. GOOD LUCK!
